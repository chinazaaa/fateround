# Coins & Shop — Art briefs

Every visual asset needed for Phase 3 (shop launch) and Phase 4 (first
paid edition), scoped so a designer or illustrator can quote and
deliver without follow-up questions. Chase the checklist at the top
of each brief before the deliverable is accepted.

## Cross-cutting constraints (apply to every brief)

- **Modes:** every asset must be legible in both the app's light and
  dark themes. Deliver a light-mode and a dark-mode variant, or one
  asset with theme-aware color tokens (SVG with `currentColor`, or a
  color palette that reads well on both).
- **Contrast:** never let a decorative color swallow a
  gameplay-critical signal (red validation tint on Sudoku, turn
  indicator, error state).
- **Trademark clean:** no branded motifs, licensed characters, real
  logos, Hasbro's classic Monopoly names anywhere. See per-brief
  notes below for edition-specific forbidden lists.
- **File formats:** vector where possible (SVG), PNG at 2x and 3x for
  raster-only. Lottie JSON for animations. Font subsets embedded or
  swapped for a system-safe stack.
- **Naming:** `{category}-{slug}-{variant}.{ext}` — e.g.
  `frame-gold-ring-light.svg`, `winner-anim-confetti.json`.
- **Delivery:** one PR per asset category (all frames in one, all
  Whot theme assets in one, etc.), with a preview grid image in the
  PR description.

## Phase 3 — Shop launch assets

### Avatar frames (4 at launch)

**What it is:** a decorative ring/border rendered around the avatar
photo (or initial). Shown wherever `Avatar` renders (lobby,
scoreboard, chat, results, profile — see
`coins-and-shop-plan.md` § "Where cosmetics render").

**Constraints:**
- Rendered at 32 px (small), 40 px (medium), and 56 px (large). Must
  read well at 40 px+; fine detail is lost at 32 px, so favor bold
  shapes.
- No fill inside — the avatar photo shows through.
- Circle geometry (avatars are circular).
- 4–6 px stroke width at 40 px baseline.

**Deliverables:**
- **`frame-gold-ring`** — solid gold with a subtle inner highlight.
  Universal appeal, "champion" vibe.
- **`frame-laurel`** — laurel-wreath decoration (top and sides,
  bottom open). Classical/prestige feel.
- **`frame-neon-cyan`** — glowing cyan ring with a soft outer bloom.
  Reads well on dark mode; needs a slightly desaturated fallback for
  light mode.
- **`frame-cosmic`** — deep-purple ring with tiny star specks.
  Playful, subtle.

Deliver as SVG with `currentColor` for the main stroke where
possible, or as two-file light/dark variants.

### Name colors (6 solids + 3 gradients at launch)

**What it is:** the treatment applied to a player's name text in
lobbies, scoreboards, chat, and everywhere else names render.

**Constraints:**
- Must pass WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1) against both light and dark app
  backgrounds. Deliver one color per slug, tuned to work in both
  modes (may need slight lightness shifts per mode — use color
  tokens rather than a single hex).
- Gradients: 2 stops max, 45° angle unless noted.
- No neon-yellow-on-white or any pairing that fails contrast checks.

**Solids (6):**
- **`name-coral`** — warm coral pink
- **`name-teal`** — muted teal
- **`name-amber`** — warm amber gold
- **`name-violet`** — mid violet
- **`name-crimson`** — deep crimson
- **`name-forest`** — deep forest green

**Gradients (3):**
- **`name-sunset`** — coral → amber, 45°
- **`name-ocean`** — teal → deep blue, 45°
- **`name-aurora`** — violet → teal → coral, 90° (subtle, not
  disco)

Deliver as a JSON spec with `light_color(s)` and `dark_color(s)` per
slug. Engineering wires the CSS variable.

### Winner animations (3 at launch)

**What it is:** a full-viewport overlay flourish that plays on the
end-of-game results screen when the winner appears. Every viewer in
the room sees the winner's equipped animation.

**Constraints:**
- Lottie JSON, ~2–3 seconds of animation
- Loops once, does not repeat
- Full-viewport overlay, transparent background
- No sound (voice chat is live during the results screen; we do not
  clash with it)
- Peak file size ≤ 100 KB per animation
- Colorblind-friendly — do not rely on red/green distinction

**Deliverables:**
- **`winner-anim-confetti`** — multi-color confetti burst from the
  bottom corners, drifting up and settling. Universal celebratory
  feel.
- **`winner-anim-fireworks`** — three staggered firework bursts in
  the upper portion of the viewport. Dramatic.
- **`winner-anim-gold-shower`** — the signature. Slow gold-coin
  cascade from top to bottom with a subtle sparkle. Ties visually to
  the coin economy — reserved feeling, not everyone will have this.

Source from LottieFiles or commission. Preview against a lobby
screenshot before shipping to confirm timing feels right.

### Card templates (2 premium at launch)

**What it is:** visual styling of the shareable results card
generated by `FinalResultsShareBlock` /
`ShareResultsCaptureHeader`. The template wraps the captured
scoreboard content with header, frame, and background art.

**Constraints:**
- Content area is variable-height (scoreboard is 2–8 rows). Design
  must accommodate any row count without breaking.
- Header + footer fixed-height slots for FateRound branding + share
  URL/QR.
- Rendered at 1080 × variable px for social-share size.
- Must remain readable when downscaled to a thumbnail (chat previews).

**Deliverables:**
- **`card-template-gold-luxe`** — dark background with gold foil
  accents on header and dividers. Serif headline font. Feels like a
  prestige award announcement.
- **`card-template-neon`** — dark background with neon cyan/magenta
  accents. Sans-serif headline. Feels like a game-show final screen.

Free default template stays as-is (no rework).

### Game themes — Whot (2 at launch)

**What it is:** card back art + deck styling + optional table felt
background for Whot rooms.

**Assets per theme:**
- Card back art (uniform across all 54 cards) — square, high-res
- Card face styling (font, color palette, number treatment) — spec,
  not per-card art
- Table felt background — full-viewport pattern or texture
- Small thumbnail (used in the theme picker) — 200 × 300 px

**Deliverables:**
- **`whot-theme-neon`** — dark card back with a glowing hexagonal
  motif; card faces with electric-blue numbers on near-black; deep
  charcoal table felt with a subtle grid.
- **`whot-theme-naija`** — Ankara / Adire textile pattern on card
  back (warm indigo/orange/red palette); card faces with a serif
  numeral in warm gold on off-white; warm-toned wooden table felt.

### Game themes — Ludo (2 at launch)

**Assets per theme:**
- Board illustration (full 40×40 square, 1080 × 1080 baseline)
- Pawn art (4 colors × 4 pawns, or 1 pawn per color × 4 colors)
- Dice art (single 6-face die, all faces)
- Home-quadrant art (4 quadrants)
- Theme picker thumbnail — 200 × 300 px

**Deliverables:**
- **`ludo-theme-wooden`** — warm walnut board, hand-carved-feel
  pawns with color-tinted wood grain, physical wooden dice with
  black dots.
- **`ludo-theme-naija`** — Ankara-pattern quadrants with distinct
  colorways per quadrant; palm-nut styled pawns (rounded, seed-like);
  brass-inlay dice.

### Game themes — Sudoku (2 at launch)

**Assets per theme:**
- Board frame / border styling
- Digit typography (font + weight)
- Palette spec (background, grid lines, selected-cell tint, error
  tint, note-mode tint)
- Theme picker thumbnail — 200 × 300 px

**Deliverables:**
- **`sudoku-theme-minimalist`** — clean sans-serif digits (Inter or
  similar), muted monochrome palette (grey scale), hairline
  dividers, subtle warm background.
- **`sudoku-theme-newsprint`** — serif digits (Playfair or similar),
  faux-newsprint paper background, "The FateRound Sudoku" header
  in old-newspaper typography, black dividers on cream.

## Phase 4 — USA edition art

**What it is:** the complete visual identity for the America edition
of Estate Kings. See `estate-kings-america-edition.md` for the full
content spec (property names, station names, utility names, corner
labels).

**Assets:**
- **Board illustration** (40-space and 48-space layouts — two
  boards): full-viewport, each color group visually distinct, USA
  motif in the center (subtle — eagle silhouette or interstate
  shield, not the flag).
- **Card back art** — Chance ("Take a Chance") and Community Chest
  ("Community Chest") card backs; USA-flavored, distinct from the
  London edition's backs.
- **Corner tile art** — PAYDAY (existing style), County Jail (bars
  and orange jumpsuit hint — playful), Roadside Diner (small diner
  icon), Squad Car (police-car icon).
- **Station icons** — Grand Central Terminal, Union Station DC, 30th
  Street Station, LA Union Station. Simplified silhouettes, not
  photorealistic.
- **Utility icons** — Hoover Dam Power (dam silhouette), Great
  Lakes Water (wave silhouette).
- **Theme picker thumbnail** — 200 × 300 px, hero board shot.

**Trademark check before delivery:**
- No property name from Hasbro's classic Monopoly list (see
  `estate-kings-america-edition.md` § "Trademark rule" for the full
  list).
- No US flag (too politically charged, and unnecessary).
- No trademarked landmark logos (Hoover Dam is a place; the dam
  silhouette is fine — do not add the "Hoover Dam" wordmark plate).
- No sports team, city seal, or federal-agency logo.

## Phase 5 — Christmas edition art

**What it is:** the complete visual identity for the Christmas
edition. See `estate-kings-christmas-edition.md` for the full
content spec.

**Assets:**
- **Board illustration** (40-space and 48-space) with snowy palette,
  ornament accents at corners, warm reds and greens as color-group
  tints while keeping the 8 color groups distinguishable.
- **Card back art** — Stocking Stuffer (was Chance) and Gift Under
  the Tree (was Community Chest) card backs; festive.
- **Corner tile art** — PAYDAY, Coal Bin (naughty-list holding
  cell), Cozy Fireside (fireplace icon), On the Naughty List
  (naughty-list scroll).
- **Station icons** — Northern Sleigh Depot, Frostwind Junction,
  Silverbell Terminal, Winterhaven Depot. Sleigh / bell / snowflake
  motifs.
- **Utility icons** — Northern Lights Co. (aurora ribbon), Frostwater
  Springs (icicle / spring motif).
- **Theme picker thumbnail** — 200 × 300 px.

**Trademark check before delivery:**
- No Rudolph, Frosty, Grinch, Charlie Brown, Elf on the Shelf,
  Nightmare Before Christmas characters or any specific named
  Christmas character.
- No Coca-Cola-style Santa illustration. Generic Santa is fine but
  design a distinct look; avoid the specific red-suit / white-beard
  proportions and coloring that Coca-Cola's is known for.
- No branded holiday products (candy brand names, retailer logos).

## Sourcing options

Any of these are fine — pick per art budget and turnaround needs.

- **In-house designer** — fastest cycle, highest consistency.
  Estimate ~40 hours of design work across the Phase 3 launch set,
  ~30 hours per edition.
- **Freelance illustrator per edition/theme pack** — clean scoping,
  ~$500–1500 per package depending on style. Fiverr, Dribbble, or
  network referrals.
- **AI-generated art with human polish** — cheapest, works well for
  card backs and patterns, less good for figurative art (pawns,
  characters). Combine with a designer for polish.
- **Lottie library** — for winner animations, LottieFiles has
  usable free and cheap-license options. Confetti and fireworks are
  well-covered; gold shower may need commissioning.

## Acceptance checklist (per asset)

- [ ] Renders correctly in light and dark app themes
- [ ] Passes contrast checks in both modes (WCAG AA for text, 3:1
      for meaningful UI)
- [ ] Trademark check per the per-brief notes
- [ ] File formats and naming per the cross-cutting constraints
- [ ] Preview grid image attached to the delivery PR
- [ ] Engineering has a spec (not just a picture) — palette, fonts,
      dimensions, states
- [ ] Colorblind-safe — no meaning conveyed by red/green only
