# FateRound Daily Challenges — Posting Kit

Captions, hashtags, and a posting schedule for growing daily players.
Link to use everywhere: **fateround.com/daily-challenges**

---

## 1. When to post (2026 data)

Times are your **audience's local time**. Default below is London time; if most of your
players are US-based (likely for English puzzles), note that **UK afternoon/evening overlaps
US morning/midday** — a global sweet spot. Check TikTok/X analytics after ~1 week and adjust.

### TikTok — post the day's game (vertical 9:16)
TikTok rewards afternoons, evenings, and weekend mornings.

| Day | Game | Best post time |
|-----|------|----------------|
| Mon | Sudoku | 1:00 pm |
| Tue | Word Hunt | 6:00–7:00 pm |
| Wed | Crossword | 9:00–10:00 pm |
| Thu | Word Search | 1:00 pm |
| Fri | Word Scramble | 6:00 pm |
| Sat | Trivia | 3:00–5:00 pm |
| Sun | Hero (all 6) | 9:00 am–1:00 pm |

If you only remember one rule: **~1 pm or ~6–8 pm daily** is reliably strong.

### X / Twitter — post the day's game (landscape 16:9 or square)
X rewards **weekday mid-mornings**; evenings and weekends underperform.

| Day | Best post time |
|-----|----------------|
| Tue / Wed / Thu (strongest) | 9:00–11:00 am |
| Mon / Fri | 9:00–10:00 am |
| Sat / Sun | Lighter — post the hero, or skip |

**Double coverage trick:** post the same day's game to X in the morning (9–11 am) and TikTok
in the afternoon/evening. Same puzzle, both platforms hit at their best time.

## 2. How to use each poster

- **Hero poster** = your anchor. Pin it to the top of both profiles, use it as your launch
  post, and re-share it on Sundays or whenever new followers arrive. Not a daily post.
- **Individual game posters** = your daily rotation. One game per day so the feed stays
  varied (each game has its own accent colour). Loop the 6 every week.
- **Sizes:** TikTok → `tiktok/` (9:16). X in-feed → `twitter/` (16:9). Universal / IG / LinkedIn → `square/` (1:1).
- **WhatsApp Status → use the vertical `tiktok/` 9:16** (fills the full status screen).
  Sharing into a chat or group → use `square/` (cleanest inline preview).

## 6. Growth tips that move the needle

- **Ask for a reply every time** — "drop your time/score 👇". Comments are the #1 signal that
  pushes a post to more people. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
- **Post consistently** at the same daily slot — followers learn when to check in.
- **Pin the hero** on both profiles and put the link in your bio (the URL on the image isn't clickable).
- **Cross-tag friends / duet-bait** — "tag someone faster than you" turns one player into two.
- **First hour matters most** — be online to engage right after posting.
